RentaVoz is the platform for honest rental reviews in Mexico. Share your experience as a tenant: rate your landlord, report property conditions, and help others make better decisions.

--- Key Features ---
- Write reviews for your past or current rentals
- Browse the map to see reviews by area
- Verify reviews with proof of residence
- Rate: landlord, property condition, location, and security
- Translate reviews to your language with one tap
- Filter and sort to find what you need

--- Why RentaVoz? ---
In Mexico, tenants often face issues like mandatory guarantors, unreturned deposits, and informal contracts. RentaVoz gives tenants a voice so the community can make informed decisions.

--- Privacy ---
We only collect what is necessary for the service. Your location is used only when you choose. See our privacy policy for details.
