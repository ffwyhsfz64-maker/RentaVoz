This Privacy Policy describes how RentaVoz ("we") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Email address (when registering with email)
  • Username you choose
  • Profile photo you voluntarily upload
  • Photos and images you attach to your reviews
  • Approximate location (only when used for address autocomplete)
  • Rental payment receipts you attach (stored securely)
  • Usage data: screens visited, reviews created

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Allow you to publish and manage your rental reviews
  • Authenticate your identity and protect your account
  • Improve the quality and relevance of content
  • Send verification emails or password reset messages
  • Detect and prevent fraudulent or abusive use

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Firebase (Google LLC) — authentication, database, and file storage
  • Google Maps Platform — map display and geocoding
  • Google Analytics for Firebase — anonymous usage analytics

These services have their own privacy policies. We do not sell your information to third parties.

4. Data Retention

We retain your information while your account is active. Upon account deletion, your personal data is erased within 30 days, except where required by law.

5. Your Rights

  • Access the data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your account and data

To exercise these rights, contact us at:

rentavozmx@gmail.com

6. Security

We apply reasonable technical measures (encryption in transit and at rest, Firestore and Storage security rules) to protect your information. However, no system is 100% secure.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy at any time. We will notify you through the app if the changes are significant.
